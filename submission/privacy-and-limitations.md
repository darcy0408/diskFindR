# Privacy And Limitations

DiscScout processes videos and exact coordinates locally by default. Do not commit personal videos, exact private coordinates, API keys, or diagnostic bundles.

Limitations:

- The landing area is an estimate, not a guarantee.
- Solo Mode cannot fully recover depth and 3D flight state.
- Trees, terrain, water, skips, rolls, and changing wind can dominate the result.
- Weather is model-derived and not an exact fairway measurement.
- The app is not a navigation, rescue, or emergency-location system.
